About This Platform

This platform was built by the Public and Private Development Centre (PPDC) to make Nigeria's primary healthcare funding data accessible, actionable, and verifiable by every citizen. The data is sourced directly from NPHCDA's official BHCPF disbursement records and validated facility list.


Data Source: NPHCDA · Federal Ministry of Health, Nigeria
Disbursement period: January – June 2025
